Overview

Understand the business workflow at a glance.

One connected workflow

Customer assistant → Orchestrator → Order database → Human approval (if needed) → Warehouse update.

1 · Customer support

Track an order or ask to cancel it. Routine questions are answered automatically; sensitive actions create a ticket.

2 · Human oversight

Review the recommendation, approve or reject. The warehouse blocks shipment while a sensitive ticket is unresolved.

3 · Warehouse protection

Check payment, order status and stock. Failed payments cannot be fulfilled.

4 · Elastic capacity

Run a local synthetic workload to see how worker allocation responds to demand.
